Columbus Blue Jackets

NHL hockey at Nationwide Arena.

The Columbus Blue Jackets bring fast-paced NHL hockey to downtown Columbus. Their games are played at Nationwide Arena in the Arena District, making them a great option for visitors who want an exciting indoor sports experience.

  • Sport: Hockey
  • League: NHL
  • Venue: Nationwide Arena
  • Best for: High-energy night games and downtown entertainment
View Team Section

Columbus Crew

Professional soccer at Lower.com Field.

The Columbus Crew are one of the most important teams in Columbus sports. Their matches are known for loud supporters, chants, and a strong soccer culture that makes game day feel different from other sports.

  • Sport: Soccer
  • League: MLS
  • Venue: Lower.com Field
  • Best for: Loud fans, chants, and outdoor match days
View Team Section

Columbus Clippers

Baseball at Huntington Park.

The Columbus Clippers offer a relaxed baseball experience close to downtown. Their games are good for families, casual fans, and visitors who want a fun sports event without the intensity of a major college or pro game.

  • Sport: Baseball
  • League: Minor League Baseball
  • Venue: Huntington Park
  • Best for: Families, summer nights, and casual fans
View Team Section

Ohio State Buckeyes

College sports and Buckeye tradition.

Ohio State athletics are a huge part of Columbus sports culture. Football Saturdays are especially important, but Ohio State also has basketball, hockey, and many other sports that bring strong school spirit to the city.

  • Sport: College athletics
  • League: NCAA
  • Venue: Ohio Stadium, Schottenstein Center, and more
  • Best for: Traditions, big crowds, and Buckeye pride
